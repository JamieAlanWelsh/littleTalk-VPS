# Chatterdillo Pilot Impact Report: Kings Furlong Infant School & Nursery

---

**1. Objective:** Improving identification of SLCN needs and distribution of high-quality, engaging language intervention support across Kings Furlong Infant School & Nursery.

---

**2. Key Targets & Progress**

**2.1 Inclusion:**

**Target:** Support 6 pupils, outlined by school-appointed SENCo (Caroline Webb).

**Result:** 7 pupils received language intervention using Chatterdillo - delivered by 3 teachers/teaching assistants/learning support assistants.

**Implication:** Long term use of Chatterdillo could lead to more pupils receiving early targeted support; reducing the risk of language needs becoming more severe, and increasing pupil's ability to engage with classroom curriculum and environments.

**Ofsted Alignment:** *“Inclusion (New Core Area)”*: Evidence that Watling Primary provides “high quality support to all”, particularly those with SEND or disadvantaged background.

---

**2.2 Staff Empowerment:**

**Target:** Increase staff confidence in identifying needs and delivering SLCN interventions

**Result:** Participating staff scored the platform an average of 4.3/5 for ease in delivering support and more confidence in ability to deliver language interventions. 2/3 survey respondents reported 2 hours of saved time per week on preparation of resources and related admin work.

A Teaching Assistant reported:

- the screener successfully helped to identify support areas,
- training videos supported the delivery of exercises,
- exercises were engaging and easy to deliver,
- and the Progress Dashboard made it easy to keep track of pupil improvements and sessions.

All responding staff reported that they would like to continue using the platform.

**Implication:** Teaching staff are able to confidently and independently use Chatterdillo to identify and address SLCN needs. Chatterdillo also saves teaching staff up to 2 hours per week, which could equate to 78 hours per school year, more than 2 full working weeks back per teacher. Time saved by using Chatterdillo may further enable support for a wider range of pupils at an early stage.

**Ofsted Alignment:** “Curriculum & Teaching”: Showing leaders are “developing staff” and ensuring staff have the right tools to address SEND needs.

---

**2.3 Quality of Education**

**Target:** Pilot demonstrates “impact” on learning or potential for impact, and evidence indicates pupils find Chatterdillo engaging.

**Result:** Participating staff scored pupil engagement levels an average of 4.3/5;

- One participating class teacher reported “improved grammar & independence” in pupil(s).
- One participating teaching assistant reported that skills built from Colourful Semantics transferred to English lessons, and Chatterdillo’s sequencing exercises helped a pupil learn how to use language to explain the order of something.
- One participating staff reported “it’s very visual and the children enjoy it”.

Pupils reported feeling ‘happy’ when asked how they felt during Chatterdillo activities, and said they would want to use Chatterdillo again “because it’s fun”.

**Implication:** Pupils had high engagement with the tool, with teaching staff reporting noticed improvements in pupil’s language development and skills. These results indicate long-term progress in pupil’s language development is achievable with consistent use of the platform.

**Ofsted Alignment:** *"Achievement & Curriculum”* Demonstrates “impact, not just intention”, Provides quantitative data and a digital audit trail into how interventions are closing the gaps.

---

**3. Pilot Impact Summary:**

Chatterdillo enabled Caroline (SENCo) to empower staff at Kings Furlong Infant School & Nursery to identify needs and deliver engaging SLCN interventions without additional training. This led to 7 pupils receiving support over the summer term.

Chaterdillo saved staff time, with reports of the majority of staff saving 2 hours per week by using Chatterdillo. The time saved by using Chatterdillo may further enable support for a wider range of pupils at an early stage.

The pilot results suggest Chatterdillo serves as an approach to address a growing area of need (SLCN), through empowerment of teaching staff in their ability to identify needs and deliver engaging and impactful interventions.

This pilot demonstrates Chatterdillo can facilitate long-term support and inclusivity for pupils with additional needs, making it significantly easier for Kings Furlong to achieve a high score for Ofsted’s new core areas.
