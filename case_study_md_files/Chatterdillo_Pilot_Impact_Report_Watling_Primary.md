# Chatterdillo Pilot Impact Report: Watling Primary

---

**1. Objective:** Improving distribution of high-quality, engaging language intervention support across Watling Primary without requiring additional training.

---

**2. Key Targets & Progress**

**2.1 Inclusion:**

**Target:**

Increase number of pupils who receive SLCN interventions over Summer Term, who would have otherwise been on a waitlist to be seen (September earliest)

**Result:** A single teaching assistant used Chatterdillo to identify and carry out SLCN interventions with 13 pupils, despite no previous specialist experience/training.

**Implication:** Long term use of Chatterdillo could lead to more pupils receiving early targeted support; reducing the risk of language needs becoming more severe, and increasing pupil's ability to engage with classroom curriculum and environments.

**Ofsted Alignment:** *“Inclusion (New Core Area)”*: Evidence that Watling Primary provides “high quality support to all”, particularly those with SEND or disadvantaged background.

---

**2.2 Staff Empowerment:**

**Target:** Increase staff confidence and ease of delivering SLCN interventions

**Result:** Participating staff scored the platform 9/10 for ease of use, and 10/10 for ease in delivering intervention material. Teaching staff reported they felt confident in delivering interventions through chatterdillo, more-so than previously used resources.

**Implication:** Support staff are able to use Chatterdillo with confidence and independence once set up with an account. One teaching assistant (Paige) proactively identified 3 further pupils who would benefit from Chatterdillo, and carried out interventions with these pupils.

**Ofsted Alignment:** “Curriculum & Teaching”: Showing leaders are “developing staff” and ensuring staff have the right tools to address SEND needs.

---

**2.3 Quality of Education**

**Target:** Pilot demonstrates “impact” on learning or potential for impact, and evidence to suggest pupils find Chatterdillo engaging.

**Result:** Participating staff scored pupil engagement levels 8/10, noting “it’s nice that I can leave momentarily (to support another pupil), and the child can continue working through an exercise without losing engagement or focus”.

Chatterdillo was only used by one teaching assistant with a select number of pupils, therefore more use would be required to show robust evidence of impact on learning.

**Implication:** Pupils engaged with the tool, especially following recent updates (new features and exercises). High engagement levels indicate long-term progress in pupil’s language development is achievable with consistent use.

**Ofsted Alignment:** *"Achievement & Curriculum”* Demonstrates “impact, not just intention”, Provides quantitative data and a digital audit trail into how interventions are closing the gaps.

---

**3. Pilot Impact Summary:**

Chatterdillo enabled Kathryn (SALT) to empower staff at Watling Primary to deliver engaging SLCN interventions without additional training. This led to 13 pupils receiving support over the summer term, who otherwise would have waited until September at the earliest.

Chaterdillo did not interfere with staffing or the school day, allowing for support staff to effectively and independently deliver interventions without needing to be released from the classroom.

With increased caseloads and needs, the waitlist for support will continue to build. The pilot results suggest Chatterdillo serves as an approach to alleviating this through distributing caseloads across more teaching assistants with minimal time commitment from coordinating staff (SENCo, SALT).

This pilot demonstrates Chatterdillo can facilitate long-term support and inclusivity for pupils with additional needs, making it significantly easier for Watling Primary’s to achieve a high score for Ofsted’s new core areas.
