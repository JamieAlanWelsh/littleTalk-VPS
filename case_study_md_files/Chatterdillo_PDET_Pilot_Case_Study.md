# Chatterdillo Pilot Impact Report: PDET Trust Schools

---

**1. Objective:** Improving access to high-quality, engaging speech and language (SLCN) intervention support across 13 Peterborough Diocese Education Trust (PDET) schools, without requiring extra staff, specialist knowledge, or complex systems — while building the evidence base schools need for Ofsted's new report card framework (effective November 2025), which rates schools on a 5-point scale across **Inclusion**, **Leadership & Governance**, and **Personal Development**.

---

**2. Key Targets & Progress**

**2.1 Reach & Participation:**

**Target:** Secure meaningful trust-wide engagement with a new SLCN intervention tool, coordinated through group feedback rather than a single-school trial.

**Result:** 13 PDET schools participated in the 6-week pilot, screening 58 children and completing 676 exercise sessions across regular group feedback sessions.

**Implication:** A single outreach effort scaling to 13 schools shows strong demand for accessible SLCN support and validates a trust-wide rollout model, rather than one-off single-school adoption.

**Ofsted Alignment:** *"Inclusion (New Core Area)"*: Evidence of coordinated, trust-level commitment to providing "high quality support to all," particularly pupils with SEND.

---

**2.2 Pupil Engagement:**

**Target:** Demonstrate that pupils engage consistently with the tool, without requiring leading questions or artificial incentives.

**Result:** 100% of pupils showed consistent engagement with Chatterdillo activities. Direct pupil quotes included *"It makes me happy!"*, *"I liked the colourful semantics because it helped me make a sentence,"* and *"I want to do it every day!"*

**Implication:** Unprompted requests to repeat sessions indicate genuine motivation, suggesting engagement gains are sustainable rather than novelty-driven.

**Ofsted Alignment:** *"Personal Development"*: Ofsted wants to see pupils engaging positively and feeling included, with clear evidence of pupil voice — gamified activities turning therapy into enjoyable play pupils actively request delivers exactly this.

---

**2.3 Staff Time-Saving:**

**Target:** Reduce staff time burden associated with preparing and delivering SLCN intervention resources.

**Result:** 78% of staff felt they saved up to 2 hours per week preparing resources. Staff reported: *"The app saves time and planning in the classroom."*

**Implication:** At scale, five staff members saving 2 hours per week each equates to roughly 100 hours saved per year per school — approximately £2,500 in staff hourly wages annually. A built-in screener automatically suggests appropriate tasks and records sessions, removing manual prep.

**Ofsted Alignment:** *"Leadership & Governance"*: Managing staff workload sustainably is now a formal consideration in the new framework — automated screening and session tracking gives leaders a lower-effort way to demonstrate this.

---

**2.4 Learning Impact:**

**Target:** Show measurable or perceived impact on pupil learning outcomes and progress.

**Result:** 89% of staff felt the tool impacted learning outcomes and progress. Staff reported: *"Children who were struggling to read and say sentences are improving."*

**Implication:** Qualitative feedback matched the quantitative data — SENCOs reported real changes in children's confidence and willingness to communicate, not just engagement metrics. Chatterdillo's research-backed, high-frequency exercises are designed to drive 3x faster progress than standard resourcing, delivered in-class or via short withdrawal sessions rather than bolted-on interventions.

**Ofsted Alignment:** *"Achievement & Curriculum / Adaptive Teaching"*: Provides quantitative data and a feedback-backed audit trail into how interventions close attainment and communication gaps, while supporting staff to adjust teaching in real time so SLCN pupils access the full curriculum alongside peers.

---

**2.5 Ease of Onboarding & Delivery:**

**Target:** Enable non-specialist staff to onboard and deliver interventions with minimal training and disruption.

**Result:** 89% of users found the tool easy to get started with. No training day was required — staff reported: *"We were up and running quickly"* and *"Screener identified correct task for child."*

**Implication:** Custom targets, exercise analytics, and screener results feed into printable, pupil-by-pupil progress dashboards with an automatic digital audit trail — no manual spreadsheets needed — so schools can evidence compliance and impact without added administrative load.

**Ofsted Alignment:** *"Inclusion"*: Gives leaders a direct answer to inspectors' key question — *"Can you show me this child's communication is improving?"* — with baseline, intervention, and outcome data per pupil.

---

**3. Trust-Level Opportunity:**

Beyond individual school results, the pilot demonstrated a case for trust-wide adoption. A bulk trust deal offers PDET consistent SLCN provision across all schools, shared data insights for trust-level SEND strategy and resource allocation, economies of scale versus individual subscriptions, and trust-wide CPD support for training and implementation.

---

**4. Pilot Impact Summary:**

Across the 6-week pilot, Chatterdillo enabled 13 PDET schools to screen 58 children and deliver 676 SLCN exercise sessions, with 100% of pupils showing consistent engagement and 89% of staff reporting a positive impact on learning outcomes. Staff also reported meaningful time savings — 78% saved up to 2 hours per week on resource preparation, worth an estimated £2,500 per year per school in staff time, with roughly 100 hours reclaimed annually per five-staff team.

The pilot was shaped through a collaborative process: regular group check-ins, direct feedback loops, and iterative feature building based on what SENCOs told the team worked and what didn't. This resulted in a tool that required no training day, integrated into existing classroom routines, and was independently praised by staff as *"intuitive, easy to use,"* requiring no specialist knowledge to deliver.

With demand demonstrated across 13 schools from a single outreach message, strong engagement and time-saving data, and a clear route to strengthening evidence against Ofsted's Inclusion, Leadership & Governance, and Personal Development judgements, the pilot supports the case for a trust-wide rollout across PDET.
