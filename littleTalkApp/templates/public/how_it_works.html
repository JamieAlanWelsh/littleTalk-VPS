Identify needs in just 10 minutes

Complete a short pupil screener that identifies language needs and recommends where to begin.

Deliver personalised interventions

Teaching assistants can immediately begin interventions using recommended activities or browse the full exercise library. We also support group interventions, so that you can make the most out of your teaching resources.

Interactive activities that keep children engaged

Children complete colourful, interactive activities tailored to their level. Chatterdillo models language throughout, while adults can reinforce learning by guiding children through each activity. Our group intervention feature also enables staff to support multiple pupils at once, helping maximise capacity and save time.

Monitor Progress

Every session is logged automatically, making it easy to monitor recent sessions and progress, and to evidence interventions. We also support re-screening, custom targets and a centralised logbook, all to keep track of progress.
