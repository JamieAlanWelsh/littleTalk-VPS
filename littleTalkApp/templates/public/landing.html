Why schools choose Chatterdillo

Support more pupils with the same capacity

Chatterdillo is easy to use and requires no specialist training, allowing teaching assistants to get started immediately. Group interventions enable staff to support multiple pupils at once, helping schools maximise limited capacity.

Save time and reduce admin

Schools using Chatterdillo have reported saving up to 3 hours each week on planning, resource preparation and admin, freeing up more time to support pupils.

Developed by Speech and Language Therapists.

Our activities are developed by Speech and Language Therapists using evidence-based approaches and recognised clinical frameworks, including Blank's Levels of Questioning, adapted for use in schools.
